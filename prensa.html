Bio corta · EspañolPara programas y presentaciones

Fabeka Lebrón es Founding Member del IESE Center for Sports Management, Académica de Honor Electa y Embajadora Extraordinaria de la Real Academia Europea de Doctores (RAED) y Founder & President de Iberoamerican Bridges. Fundadora de Barna Business School, de la Barça Academy República Dominicana y del MIC Football Punta Cana, une la academia, la empresa y el deporte en el ecosistema iberoamericano. Dedica esta etapa de su vida a la diplomacia económica, la gobernanza y la mentoría de personas con talento y propósito.

Short bio · EnglishFor international programmes

Fabeka Lebrón is a Founding Member of the IESE Center for Sports Management, Elected Honorary Academician and Extraordinary Ambassador of the Royal European Academy of Doctors (RAED), and Founder & President of Iberoamerican Bridges. Founder of Barna Business School, Barça Academy Dominican Republic and MIC Football Punta Cana, she brings together academia, business and sport across the Iberoamerican ecosystem. She devotes this chapter of her life to economic diplomacy, governance and mentoring people of talent and purpose.
